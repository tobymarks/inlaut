inlaut for Legal & Healthcare: Local Dictation on Mac and Windows

Attorneys, notary offices, tax advisors and physicians work with confidential information. inlaut recognises dictation locally on Mac and, as a Windows beta, on x64 PCs: no cloud recognition, no account and open source. Your IT team should review local data handling and the beta’s limits before deployment.

Note: This page describes the technical architecture of inlaut. It does not constitute legal counsel. Compliance evaluations rest with your organization’s legal or data protection officers.

The Dilemma of Cloud Dictation in Confidential Fields

When dictating client counsel, court briefs, or sensitive medical records (GDPR Art. 9 / HIPAA), transmitting unencrypted voice audio to remote cloud servers carries severe compliance risks:

  • Legal Privilege & Professional Secrecy: Disclosing confidential client matters or health records to third-party cloud infrastructure creates substantial regulatory and liability vulnerabilities.
  • Special Category Data (GDPR Art. 9): Medical histories, diagnoses, and litigation files demand strict protections that generic AI SaaS providers rarely fulfill.
  • Permanent Audio Retention: Many consumer dictation apps default to saving raw audio files indefinitely on remote servers or unencrypted local directories.

How inlaut Protects Confidentiality by Design

inlaut sends no audio or text to a cloud service for speech recognition:

  • 100% On-Device Processing: Speech recognition runs entirely on local hardware: Parakeet Ultra on Mac, German and English FastConformer models in the Windows beta.
  • Zero Cloud Intermediaries: inlaut runs no transcription servers. Speech recognition functions fully without an active internet connection—even inside strictly air-gapped practice networks.
  • No Audio History or Retention: Normal dictation is recognised in memory and its audio is discarded afterwards. The microphone runs during a started dictation, including hands-free mode, or an explicitly started test. The Windows beta keeps the last dictation in memory and can save audio and recognised text to disk when test captures are explicitly enabled. These files are not sent automatically.
  • No DPA Required: Local recognition sends no dictations to the developer. As we understand it, this involves no processing on your behalf. Your team must assess the legal requirements and the use of the destination app, clipboard and any diagnostic material you choose to share.

Future-Proof Independence Through Open Source

For legal practices and clinics, software vendor lock-in represents a critical operational risk. Many firms have experienced legacy vendors acquiring established tools, inflating license prices, or forcing unwanted cloud migrations.

  • Zero Vendor Lock-in: inlaut is licensed under the GNU General Public License (GPL-3.0). The codebase belongs to no single corporate entity and remains permanently open.
  • Complete Auditability: IT security teams, data protection officers, and external auditors can inspect every line of code on GitHub. There are no proprietary black boxes, hidden telemetry beacons, or unauthorized model training on your text.
  • Perpetual Operational Continuity: Even if upstream development were ever discontinued, your internal IT or third-party service provider can fork, adapt, and run inlaut indefinitely.
  • Free Instead of Four-Figure Licenses: While traditional professional dictation packages (such as Dragon Legal or Dragon Medical) cost upwards of $800 to $2,500+ per seat, inlaut is 100% free forever—even for commercial firm and hospital deployments.

Seamless Practice Workflow Integration

inlaut adapts directly to your established desktop environment without disrupting existing tooling:

  • Types Directly at the Cursor: inlaut inserts text into the selected field, for example in a word processor or email app. Test your case-management or clinical software; compatibility with individual specialist applications is not guaranteed. On Windows, insertion into apps running as administrator can be restricted.
  • Custom Replacements for Terminology: Define custom substitutions for statutory abbreviations (e.g. GDPR, HIPAA, USC, CFR), Latin phrases, or complex medical diagnoses.
  • Voice-Triggered Snippets: Insert multi-line disclaimers, standardized clauses, or firm signatures with a single spoken trigger phrase. Custom dictionaries and snippets can be exported and distributed across your team.

macOS and the Windows beta

macOS requires Apple Silicon and macOS 26 or later. Windows beta 0.7.0-beta.10 supports Windows 10/11 (x64) and bundles both local speech models: English or German, without mixed-language dictation. File transcription and Apple Intelligence are Mac features. Windows 11 acceptance testing is still pending.

The Windows installer is unsigned; Windows may warn about or block installation. Updates are installed manually after quitting inlaut. Read the installation notes and beta limits and test your applications before using confidential data.

FAQ for Practice & Clinical IT Teams

Does inlaut initiate outbound network connections?

On Mac, the speech model is downloaded once from Hugging Face; the Windows beta bundles both models and can download missing German model files. A missing English model requires reinstalling the bilingual installer. Optional update checks contact inlaut.de; new versions come from GitHub. Updates to the unsigned Windows beta are installed manually through the website. These requests do not contain dictations. Diagnostic material is shared only if you explicitly submit it yourself.

Can inlaut be deployed on isolated or air-gapped networks?

Recognition works offline once the app and models are ready. Mac downloads its model first; the Windows installer bundles both models. Review installation, update procedures and IT policies before deploying to isolated networks.

How does the clipboard handle privileged text?

On Mac, inlaut inserts text through the clipboard and then restores its previous contents. Windows uses direct insertion by default. The optional clipboard mode replaces the previous contents and leaves the latest dictation there. Windows history and cloud sync are disabled for that entry; third-party clipboard managers must respect the flags themselves. The destination app determines what happens to text after insertion.

Are enterprise support contracts or SLAs available?

inlaut is an independent open-source project and does not offer paid SLAs. Detailed specifications for security and network architecture are documented openly on our inlaut at work page.
