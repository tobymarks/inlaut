Superwhisper Alternative for Mac

inlaut is a free, open-source dictation app for macOS. Both apps type speech straight into any active text field. The key difference: inlaut uses the modern Parakeet Fast-Conformer model by default, does not lock real-time performance behind subscriptions, and does not maintain an audio history on disk.

As of October 14, 2026. Authored by the creator of inlaut. Statements regarding Superwhisper are verified from vendor documentation and referenced below.

At a Glance

  • Parakeet included for free: In Superwhisper, fast transducer models like Parakeet require the paid Pro plan. inlaut ships Parakeet Ultra by default at zero cost.
  • No subscription, no lifetime paywall: inlaut is 100% free software (GPL-3.0). Superwhisper Pro is priced at $8.49/month, $84.99/year, or $249.99 for a lifetime license.
  • Zero audio logs: Superwhisper stores recordings locally indefinitely by default. inlaut keeps no audio files and no transcription history.
  • Lightweight footprint: inlaut idles at only ~50–60 MB RAM in your menu bar.

Where Superwhisper Excels

Superwhisper is a versatile, feature-rich power tool designed for users who want to feed dictated speech through Large Language Models for automated rewriting:

  • Custom AI Modes & System Prompts: Superwhisper Pro lets you define prompts that summarize notes, format code snippets, or rewrite emails.
  • Multiplatform: Superwhisper supports Windows, iOS, and Android alongside macOS. inlaut is currently focused strictly on Apple Silicon Macs.
  • File Transcription & Meeting Mode: Superwhisper Pro includes integrated features for transcribing pre-recorded audio files and meetings.
  • Over 100 Languages: With various Whisper model options, Superwhisper supports a wide global language spectrum.

Why Mac Users Choose inlaut

1. Parakeet without a paywall: To get fast, near-zero latency transcription in Superwhisper, users must subscribe to Pro ($8.49/mo). Its free tier is limited to Whisper, which pauses noticeably after every sentence and can hallucinate during silence. inlaut includes Parakeet Ultra by default and free forever.

2. True privacy with zero retention: Superwhisper saves every voice recording to your drive indefinitely by default (“Keep recordings: Forever”). inlaut processes audio strictly in volatile memory and discards it immediately after typing. No voice database is created on your machine.

3. Ultra-lightweight: Running at ~50–60 MB RAM in the menu bar, inlaut does not impact battery life, thermal throttling, or system memory.

4. Seamless bilingual German & English: Phrases mixing both languages (such as technical terminology in conversation) are recognized smoothly without manual mode switching.

How to Switch from Superwhisper to inlaut

  1. Download inlaut: Open the DMG file and move inlaut into your Applications folder.
  2. Setup: On first launch, inlaut downloads the on-device model package once (~630 MB).
  3. Choose your trigger: Set your preferred shortcut or hold the 🌐 fn key to dictate. Releasing the key types the text instantly.
  4. Import custom vocabulary: Add names or specific technical jargon under inlaut’s Replacements settings.

Both applications can remain installed simultaneously on your Mac. Simply assign different hotkeys to compare their speed and accuracy side-by-side.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a completely free alternative to Superwhisper?

Yes. inlaut is 100% free under the GPL-3.0 license with no limits, accounts, or in-app purchases. Superwhisper provides a limited Free plan restricted to Whisper models and up to 2 modes, while fast Transducer models and cloud features require Superwhisper Pro ($8.49/mo or $249.99 lifetime).

Why is inlaut faster than Superwhisper Free?

Superwhisper Free relies on local OpenAI Whisper models, which process audio in chunks and incur 1 to 3 seconds of decoding delay when dictating. inlaut uses the streaming Parakeet Ultra FastConformer model natively accelerated on the Apple Neural Engine, typing text under 0.1 seconds after releasing the key.

Does inlaut store recordings or an audio history?

No. inlaut never stores audio recordings or transcription history. Superwhisper saves recordings locally on disk indefinitely by default (“Keep recordings for: Forever”) unless changed manually in settings.

Is inlaut open source?

Yes. The complete source code of inlaut is licensed under GPL-3.0 and available on GitHub. Superwhisper is closed-source, proprietary software.

Does inlaut support English and German in the same sentence?

Yes. inlaut's “German + English” mode fluently handles bilingual sentences with technical terms without requiring manual language switching.
