Room for your thoughts.

inlaut stays in the background. And it’s there when you have something to say.

Your computer listens. Nobody else.

Speech recognition runs on your computer. Normal dictation neither keeps recordings and text as a history nor sends them anywhere. Windows test captures are only saved when you explicitly enable them.

English, German, or both.

On Mac, inlaut understands English and German, even mixed, using Parakeet Ultra. Alternatively, use local Apple speech recognition for a selected language. The Windows beta offers separate local models for German and English. Your speech stays on your device.

Right where you’re writing.

Email, note or document: your dictation lands straight in the active text field. Try the Windows beta with your own apps first; text insertion can behave differently between applications.

Your words. Your spelling.

Custom replacements fix recurring mistakes. Say “new line” and “new paragraph” to structure your text as you speak.

Quietly in the background.

A small indicator while you speak. A menu in the menu bar on Mac or the system tray on Windows. These images show the Mac version.

Hold. Speak.
Let go.

One key is all it takes. inlaut does the rest on your computer.

  1. 1

    Set up on your computer.

    Mac: Open the DMG, drag inlaut into Applications, then launch it from there. The app downloads the speech model (around 630 MB) and walks you through microphone and text insertion permissions.

    Windows: Read the beta guidance and run the EXE installer. It installs for your user account, with both speech models included. The beta is unsigned, so Windows may display a security warning.

  2. 2

    Hold your key and speak.

    Choose your shortcut on either platform. On Mac, you can also use the 🌐 fn key and double-tap it for hands-free dictation. On Windows, choose in settings whether to hold the key or press it to toggle recording.

  3. 3

    Let go. The text is there.

    inlaut turns your words into text and inserts it at the cursor. In hands-free mode, another key press ends the recording.

Frequently asked questions

What inlaut can do, what it needs and where your data stays.

Is inlaut free?

Yes. inlaut is free, with no subscription, no account and no ads. The source code is open source under the GPL-3.0 license and lives on GitHub.

Does dictation work without internet?

Yes. Speech recognition runs on your computer. On Mac, inlaut downloads the speech model on first launch (around 630 MB); the Windows beta includes both speech models in the installer. Downloads and update checks need internet; dictation does not.

Are my recordings sent to a cloud?

No. Your voice is turned into text on your computer. Normal dictation neither sends recordings or text anywhere nor keeps a history. Optional Windows test captures are only saved locally when explicitly enabled. The microphone is active during dictation or an explicitly started microphone test.

Which apps can I dictate into?

inlaut inserts text at the cursor, for example in Mail, Notes, Word, Outlook, a browser or a chat window. On Mac, this needs a one-time permission under Accessibility. Without it, the text stays on the clipboard. Try the Windows beta with your own apps first: insertion is not yet reliable everywhere. More about the beta’s limits.

How do I start dictating?

On Mac: Hold your key combination or the 🌐 fn key, speak and let go. For longer texts, double-tap fn and dictate hands-free. Another key press ends the recording. On Windows, choose your shortcut and recording mode in the settings.

Which languages does inlaut support?

On Mac: English and German, including both mixed in one sentence. The Windows beta offers German or English dictation and interface, selected in settings. Mixed-language dictation is not supported on Windows. The two versions do not yet have identical features.

How well does inlaut understand English and technical terms?

On Mac, inlaut uses Parakeet Ultra by default; the Windows beta uses separate FastConformer models for English and German. On both platforms, custom replacements can correct recurring recognition errors. Check technical terms, names and numbers before sharing your text.

Can I dictate line breaks and paragraphs?

Yes. Say “new line” or “new paragraph”, and inlaut inserts the break right there.

How is this different from macOS dictation?

macOS has its own dictation. inlaut is an alternative with its own speech model, custom replacements, commands for lines and paragraphs and a key of your choice. To set up the built-in feature, see our guide to Mac dictation, or compare them directly in our dictation app comparison.

Which computers does inlaut support?

Mac: Apple silicon (M1 or later), macOS 26 or later. Windows beta: Windows 10 or 11 on an Intel or AMD x64 PC. The Windows installer is unsigned; Windows 11 acceptance testing is still in progress. There is no native Windows ARM, Linux, iPhone or iPad version.

Can inlaut transcribe recordings too?

On Mac: Yes. “Transcribe File …” in the menu turns an audio or video file into text, for example the recording of a meeting or a voice message. The result appears in its own window, with time stamps if you like, and can be copied or saved. This, too, happens only on your Mac. inlaut does not tell speakers apart. The Windows beta does not yet offer file transcription.

Which microphone does inlaut use?

On Mac: You choose it in the setting “Record with”. With “Automatic”, inlaut uses the Mac’s own microphone while a Bluetooth headset is connected, because headsets take over a second to start recording. You can also follow the macOS setting or pick one microphone. On Windows, you can choose and test a microphone in settings too; the automatic Bluetooth-specific rule is not available there.

Does inlaut remove “um” and slips of the tongue?

On Mac: If you want it to. With the setting “Tidy up dictations”, inlaut leaves out filler sounds such as “uh” and words you said twice by accident. With “Resolve self-corrections (beta)”, “on Monday, no, on Tuesday” simply becomes “on Tuesday”. For that, Apple Intelligence on your Mac checks whether you really corrected yourself; nothing is sent. In both cases inlaut only deletes words and does not rephrase anything. Both settings are off at first and are not available in the Windows beta.

Can I insert snippets?

Yes. Under “Snippets” you store longer texts you need again and again, such as a signature or bank details. Say the phrase on its own and inlaut writes the text. Replacements and snippets can be exported together and imported on another computer running inlaut.

Is inlaut available for Windows, iPhone or iPad?

The native Windows beta is available as an unsigned download. See the Windows beta page for Windows security warning guidance and requirements. inlaut is not available for iPhone or iPad.

How do I install inlaut?

On Mac, open the DMG and drag inlaut to Applications. First launch guides you through the model download and permissions. The Windows beta uses an EXE installer for your user account. The installer is unsigned; Windows may show a security warning during installation.
