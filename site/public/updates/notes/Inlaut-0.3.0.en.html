inlaut 0.3.0

  • New speech recognition: Parakeet Ultra now runs on your Mac's Neural Engine. In our measurements it makes fewer mistakes than before, in English, in German and in mixed sentences.
  • Much faster and lighter: a ten-minute dictation is text in about three seconds. Idle, inlaut needs around 60 MB of memory instead of more than 600 MB, and hardly any processor time.
  • One model for English, Deutsch and Deutsch + English: changing the language no longer downloads anything.
  • After the update, inlaut downloads the new model once in the background (around 630 MB). Until it is ready, you keep dictating with Apple's speech recognition. The previous models are removed.
  • Replacements can include the word before a term, so a rule only applies where you mean it.
  • Sign-offs before “new line” keep their comma (“Best regards,”).
