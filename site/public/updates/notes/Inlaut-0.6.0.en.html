inlaut 0.6.0

  • Per-app rules: under “Per-App Rules” you add apps where a finished sentence is wrong, such as Terminal, a code editor or a chat. There inlaut leaves out the full stop at the end and starts with a lowercase letter. You can turn off either rule per app. What counts is the app in front when you start dictating.
  • What belongs stays: the dot of single-letter abbreviations such as “e.g.” or “a.m.”, the capitals in words such as “USA”, “JavaScript” or “I”, and your own replacements at the start. Snippets come out exactly as you saved them.
  • “Resolve self-corrections (beta)” now also finds German number words such as “zwanzig, nein, dreißig” and corrected German dates, where the dot after the day used to end the sentence: “am 12. September, nein, am 12. Oktober” becomes “am 12. Oktober”. Months and weekdays are now found reliably on macOS 26 too.
