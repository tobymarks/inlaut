inlaut 0.2.0

  • inlaut now speaks English. Choose your language under Settings → Speech Recognition: Deutsch, English or Deutsch + English. It sets the speech model, the voice commands and the interface language.
  • English uses NVIDIA Parakeet TDT 0.6B v2, the most accurate model we measured for native English speakers.
  • Deutsch + English is for everyone who dictates both, even in one sentence. The German model handles English with a German accent at least as well as the English-only model, so no extra download is needed.
  • English voice commands: “new line” and “new paragraph” insert line breaks and paragraphs.
  • When you switch languages, you keep dictating with the current model until the new one is ready; then the old one is deleted. If you switch often, “Keep both models” leaves both on your Mac and switches from the menu in a few seconds.
