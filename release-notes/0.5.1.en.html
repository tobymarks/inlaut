inlaut 0.5.1

  • “New line” and “new paragraph” now also work at the start and end of a dictation, where the line break used to be dropped. Saying just “new paragraph” inserts a paragraph at the cursor.
  • German ß: compound words with “ss” at the joint stay correct, such as “Einkaufspassage”, “Gleistrasse”, “Saugrüssel” and “Vliesstoff”. Until now, one of them could become “Einkaufspaßage”.
  • A “...” at the end of a short last line stays whole instead of being cut to “..”.
  • “Tidy up dictations” keeps spoken line breaks, even when a filler word or repeat right next to them is left out.
