inlaut 0.6.1

  • Dictate again sooner: the next dictation can start right after the text is pasted. Until now, a key press in the first 0.4 seconds after it was lost. Your clipboard comes back as before.
  • After a pause of about a minute, the first dictation took noticeably longer. inlaut now gets the model ready while you are still speaking.
  • German ß: inlaut no longer turns “ss” into “ß”. The model writes “ß” correctly itself, and the old rule only caught English words and names (“gross margin”, “Herr Weiss”). The switch is now called “ss instead of ß” and is for Swiss spelling (“Strasse”, “gross”); it is on from the start in Switzerland and Liechtenstein. The previous setting is not carried over.
  • Pasting also works with keyboard layouts that have the V in a different place, such as Dvorak.
  • If the connection drops while the speech model is downloading, inlaut continues where it stopped instead of starting over.
  • A forgotten recording ends by itself after 30 minutes. If macOS does not accept a shortcut, inlaut now says so.
