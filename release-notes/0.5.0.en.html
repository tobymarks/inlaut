inlaut 0.5.0

  • Snippets: under “Snippets” you can store longer texts you need again and again, such as a signature or bank details. Say the phrase on its own and inlaut writes the text. Replacements and snippets can be exported together and imported on another Mac.
  • New setting “Tidy up dictations”: inlaut leaves out filler sounds such as “uh” and words you said twice by accident. The setting is off at first.
  • Resolve self-corrections (beta): “on Monday, no, on Tuesday” becomes “on Tuesday”. inlaut finds such places itself and lets Apple Intelligence on your Mac check whether you really corrected yourself. Nothing is sent. The setting appears below “Tidy up dictations” and needs Apple Intelligence.
